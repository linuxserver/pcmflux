Expand description

Python-facing mic-playback configuration read by AudioPlayback.start.

Defaults match the client mic wire (S16LE / mono / 24 kHz). max_buffer_bytes is the single byte bound on the drop-oldest playback queue (~2 s at 24 kHz mono s16), and device_name accepts str | bytes | None.
